# 5.1 Help Me Carry

Source: RoboCup@Home Rulebook 2025.1, Final, Rev 2025-04-28, section 5.1, pp. 39-41.
The rulebook is the source of truth. This page restates it and says what slice 1 simulates.

## Goal

The robot helps the operator carry a bag from a car parked outside to the starting location.

- Main goal: bring a bag from the car.
- Optional goals: (1) re-reach the starting location, (2) follow the queue on the way back.
- Focus: person following, navigation in unmapped environments, social navigation.

## Setup

- Locations: the test is outside the arena in a public space. The robot starts at a predefined location.
- People: the operator stands in front of the robot.
- Objects: at least two bags near the car, a small object on the ground, a hard-to-see object, retractable barriers.
- Uncontrolled environment: other people may walk by or stand around during the whole task.

## Procedure

1. Following the operator. The robot informs the operator when it is ready to follow. The operator walks naturally to the car, then points at the bag.
2. Obstacles. The robot meets 4 obstacles in arbitrary order: (a) small object on the ground, (b) hard-to-see object, (c) a crowd blocking the path, (d) a small area blocked with retractable barriers.
3. Picking up the bag. The robot picks up the bag the operator points at.
4. Bag delivery. The robot takes the bag to the starting location. It may place the bag on the floor.
5. Optional goal, following the queue. After the robot reaches the car, a few people from the crowd return to the start in a queue. The robot may join the queue in a way that looks natural to them.

## Additional rules

1. Car location. There is no real car. A fixed location outside the arena is the car location.
2. Reaching the car. The robot can reach the car only by following the operator. The location is unknown before the test.
3. Deus ex Machina. Score is reduced when the robot loses the operator and finds them again through:
   1. natural interaction (waving, calling), -50
   2. unnatural interaction (raising both hands, jumping), -100
   3. coming back (asking the operator to step back in front of the robot), -150
   4. touching the robot (pulling its hand), -200

   Penalties apply only if the robot keeps scoring after the interaction. They can repeat, up to a total of 250 points.

## Time limit

The maximum time for this test is **5:00 minutes**.

## How slice 1 interprets the rulebook

These are choices of this project, not rulebook text.

- "Reached the car" is judged by the referee code: the robot is within 3 m of the car point. The robot code never reads the car point.
- "Perceived the correct bag" is awarded when the bag the task perceived matches the bag the operator points at. The task says which bag it sees in the same step. The referee does not check the speech log.
- "Re-reached the start" needs the robot within 1 m of the start, after the car was reached, with the target bag within 1 m of the start too.
- Putting the bag down at the start on purpose is not a drop. A drop happens only through `manipulation.slip()`, which tests use.
- The Deus ex Machina cap (-250) is implemented in the score sheet. The "only if the robot keeps scoring" condition is not modelled, because the simulation never triggers a Deus ex Machina event.
